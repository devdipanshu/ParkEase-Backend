List Your Parking Lot on ParkEase

Turn your idle parking space into a steady revenue stream — no complicated setup required.

How It Works for Owners

  1. Register — Create a Manager account in under 2 minutes.
  2. Add Your Lot — Enter your lot's name, location, total capacity, and hourly rate.
  3. Define Spots — Set up individual spots with types (car, bike, EV) and special pricing.
  4. Go Live — Your lot is visible to all ParkEase drivers immediately.
  5. Track & Earn — Monitor bookings, occupancy, and revenue from your dashboard.

What You Get

  • Real-time occupancy dashboard — know what's happening at your lot live
  • Automated booking management — no manual check-ins needed
  • Revenue reports with daily, weekly, and monthly breakdowns
  • Analytics on peak hours, utilization rates, and revenue trends
  • Instant payment settlement — cash and online payments both supported

Pricing for Lot Owners

ParkEase charges a flat 5% platform fee on each booking made through the app. There are no monthly fees, no setup costs, and no lock-in contracts. You only pay when you earn.

Who Is This For?

  • Standalone parking lots in commercial or residential areas
  • Apartment complexes with unused visitor parking
  • Mall or office building parking managed by facilities teams
  • Any space that can be safely parked in and has at least 2 spots
