Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Effective from: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly — such as your name, email address, phone number, and vehicle details when you register or update your profile. We also collect location data (latitude/longitude) when you search for nearby parking lots, and usage data such as bookings, payment history, and notifications. We do not store raw GPS coordinates beyond the duration of a search session.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the ParkEase parking service
  • To process bookings and payments securely via Razorpay
  • To send booking confirmations and relevant notifications
  • To improve our platform based on aggregate usage patterns
  • To respond to your support queries and feedback

3. Payment Data

All payments are processed by Razorpay. ParkEase does not store your card number, CVV, or UPI PIN. We only store the Razorpay transaction ID and payment status for booking records. Razorpay is PCI-DSS compliant and your payment credentials remain secure under their platform.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only with:

  • Parking lot owners (managers) — limited to your booking details for that specific lot
  • Razorpay — for payment processing
  • Legal authorities — only when required by law

5. Data Retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account is active. Booking and payment records are kept for up to 3 years for accounting and dispute resolution purposes. You may request deletion of your account by contacting us at support@parkease.com.

6. Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt out of non-essential notifications

7. Contact Us

For any privacy-related queries, write to us at support@parkease.com or call +91-7452976379. We aim to respond within 48 hours.
