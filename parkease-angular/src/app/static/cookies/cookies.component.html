Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Effective from: January 2025

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help us remember your preferences and improve your experience on ParkEase.

Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

Required for the platform to function. These include your authentication token (JWT) stored in localStorage, and session identifiers. You cannot opt out of these.

Functional Cookies

Remember your preferences such as search radius and booking type. These make the platform more convenient to use on return visits.

Third-party Cookies (Razorpay)

When you initiate a payment, Razorpay's checkout may set cookies for fraud prevention and session management. These are governed by Razorpay's Privacy Policy.

Local Storage

ParkEase stores your JWT token, user role, full name, and user ID in browser localStorage to keep you logged in across sessions. This data is cleared automatically when you sign out.

Managing Cookies

You can clear cookies and localStorage data at any time through your browser settings. Clearing authentication data will log you out of ParkEase. Note that disabling essential cookies will prevent the platform from functioning correctly.
