About ParkEase

Founded in 2024  ·  Bangalore, India

Our Mission

ParkEase was built to solve one of the most frustrating everyday problems — finding a parking spot. We connect drivers with parking lot owners in real time, making urban parking seamless, transparent, and stress-free.

What We Do

  • Real-time parking spot availability across registered lots
  • Instant booking with pre-paid and walk-in options
  • Secure payments via Razorpay or cash
  • Lot management tools for parking lot owners
  • Analytics and revenue dashboards for managers and admins

Our Story

ParkEase started as a college project by a team of computer science students tired of circling the same block for 20 minutes looking for parking. What began as a simple idea grew into a full-stack microservices platform — with a Spring Boot backend, Angular frontend, and real-time notifications — designed to scale across any city.

Technology

Our platform is built on a modern microservices architecture using Spring Boot, Angular, MySQL, Redis, and RabbitMQ. Services communicate through Eureka service discovery, and all traffic flows through a centralized API Gateway — ensuring reliability, security, and speed.
